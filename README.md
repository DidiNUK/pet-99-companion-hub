## 🧭 A Different Kind of Pet Companion

Imagine a sprawling, sun-drenched archipelago where every island hides a new creature, a new coin vein, a new reason to keep exploring. That is the world Pet Simulator 99 invites you into — and the **Pet Simulator 99 Companion Atlas** is the quiet cartographer standing beside you, notebook in hand, mapping the terrain you have already covered and sketching the routes still ahead.

Where other tools shout numbers at you, the Atlas *narrates* them. It watches your roster grow, tracks the coins your pets bring home, layers your farming loops onto a living map, and quietly suggests where the next hour of play might feel most rewarding. It is less a dashboard and more a field journal — one that happens to be very good at arithmetic.

Built for the 2026 season of the game, this toolkit folds pet collection science, team composition planning, inventory housekeeping, zone progression, and gameplay analytics into a single responsive workspace that runs wherever you do. Whether you are a weekend explorer or a dedicated completionist chasing the last few entries in a massive index, the Atlas keeps your journey legible.

## 🚀 Why the Atlas Exists

Most companion utilities treat your save data like a spreadsheet. The Atlas treats it like a **story with chapters**. Every pet you acquire is a character entry. Every zone you clear is a milestone. Every farming route you refine is a paragraph you have rewritten until it flows.

This repository is the home of that philosophy: a community-driven, privacy-respecting, offline-first toolkit that respects your time and your hardware. Nothing phones home. Nothing nags you to upgrade. You open it, you glance, you learn, you close it, and you go back to playing.

## ✨ Feature Constellation

### 🧺 Pet Collection & Index Tracking
- **Living Pet Index** — every creature you have met, sorted by rarity band, elemental family, and acquisition zone.
- **Missing-Entry Radar** — surfaces the gaps in your index so you know exactly which zones deserve another visit.
- **Duplicate Analytics** — highlights overstocked pets that are ideal candidates for trading or combining routines.
- **Golden & Variant Flags** — visually distinguishes standard, golden, and event-limited forms at a glance.

### 🧠 Team Composition Studio
- **Synergy Scoring** — rates candidate squads against damage output, coin yield, and survivability weights you control.
- **Role Balancing** — flags teams that lean too heavily on one archetype and suggests balanced alternatives.
- **Loadout Snapshots** — save named team presets for different activities: boss runs, coin farming, event chasing.
- **Swap Simulator** — preview the effect of replacing a single pet before you commit in-game.

### 🎒 Inventory & Storage Housekeeping
- **Capacity Heatmap** — shows how close you are to storage ceilings across categories.
- **Auto-Tag Rules** — label pets by trait, zone, or personal notes using simple rule strings.
- **Clutter Report** — a gentle weekly digest of items you have not interacted with in a while.
- **Export & Snapshot** — take portable snapshots of your inventory for personal record-keeping.

### 🗺️ Zone & Progression Mapping
- **Progress Ladder** — a vertical view of zone unlocks, showing which thresholds remain.
- **Bottleneck Detector** — identifies the single zone currently slowing your overall advancement.
- **Recommended Detours** — suggests side activities when a mainline zone feels grindy.
- **Biome Notes** — user-written annotations that travel with each zone entry.

### 🌾 Farming Route Planner
- **Route Builder** — chain zones into custom loops and estimate yield per cycle.
- **Timing Profiles** — save fast loops, balanced loops, and relaxed loops for different moods.
- **Yield Estimator** — projected coin and item throughput based on your recent session data.
- **Session Comparison** — line up two farming sessions side by side to see which route actually performed better.

### 📊 Gameplay Analytics
- **Session Timeline** — a scrollable ribbon of your recent play sessions with key events marked.
- **Efficiency Trends** — seven-day and thirty-day trendlines for coins, items, and index growth.
- **Milestone Log** — automatic entries when you cross meaningful thresholds.
- **Personal Best Board** — celebrate your own records without comparing to strangers.

### 🎛️ Interface & Experience
- **Responsive Layout** — panels reflow smoothly from ultrawide monitors to compact laptop screens.
- **Multilingual Support** — fourteen interface locales, switchable without a restart.
- **Theme Palette** — light, dusk, and midnight schemes tuned for long sessions.
- **Keyboard-First Navigation** — every panel reachable without a mouse.
- **Reduced Motion Mode** — calmer transitions for users who prefer a stiller interface.

### 🛟 Reliability & Support
- **Local-First Storage** — your data lives on your machine in a readable format.
- **Crash-Resilient Autosave** — snapshots taken at short intervals so nothing is lost.
- **24/7 Customer Support** — a rotating volunteer desk answers questions around the clock.
- **Guided Onboarding** — a short, skippable walkthrough for first-time users.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

The Atlas was drawn with three quiet principles in mind.

**Legibility over density.** A wall of numbers is easy to build and hard to read. Every panel in the Atlas asks a single question and answers it clearly before moving on.

**Your data is yours.** There is no account, no telemetry pipeline, no hidden sync. The Atlas reads local snapshots you provide and writes local reports you own.

**Calm by default.** The interface avoids flashing counters and aggressive notifications. Progress is shown, not shouted.

## 🧪 Example Usage Scenarios

**The Collector's Sunday.** You open the Atlas on a quiet afternoon, glance at the Missing-Entry Radar, and see three gaps concentrated in two zones. You queue the zones as a relaxed loop, play for ninety minutes, and come back to watch the index climb by two entries.

**The Founder's Friday.** Before a big boss attempt, you spin up the Team Composition Studio, load a saved loadout, and run three swap simulations. The synergy score nudges you toward a different support pet. You commit, and the run goes smoother than last week's.

**The Analyst's Month-End.** Thirty days of session data roll into the Ledger. The efficiency trendline shows a steady climb, the milestone log lists four personal bests, and the bottleneck detector has quietly retired three zones from the warning list.

**The Multilingual Household.** Two players share one machine but prefer different languages. Each launches the Atlas with their own locale preset, and the interface greets them in their chosen tongue without a single settings dive.

## 🔒 Privacy & Data Handling

The Atlas operates on the principle of **local gravity** — data falls onto your machine and stays there.

- No user accounts are required to use any feature.
- No usage statistics are transmitted anywhere.
- Snapshot files are stored in plain, human-readable formats.
- Imported data can be purged with a single action from the Settings Hub.
- Community-contributed translations are the only external assets, and they are downloaded only when you explicitly opt in.
